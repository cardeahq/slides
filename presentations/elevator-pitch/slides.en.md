# Cardea

**Stay in control of what your AI agents do with your data.**

AI agents read your files, use external tools and act on your behalf, often without human approval. Few companies really know what they do with the data. For a healthcare facility or a law firm, a single leak breaches medical or professional confidentiality.

Cardea installs inside your infrastructure, between your agents and the outside world. It controls their actions in real time, blocks anything risky (for example, sending a patient file to an unauthorized service) and keeps a record of everything. Sensitive data is filtered before it leaves your environment.

An online console, hosted in Europe, shows which agents are in use, what they do and what was blocked. It also helps you meet compliance requirements with audit reports.

Cardea's core is open source: you can audit it, with no black box.
